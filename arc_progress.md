# CURRENT ARC SO FAR (Arc 1, chapters 1-30)
Lin Chen, reborn at 31 into his 15-year-old self in September 2006, lives with Grandma in Qingshui and is rebuilding his life around the game AotA. He has gathered Team Starlight: Big Bird, Su Wen (the counter), Monk (the carry), Fang Hu (the cheerful kid with a heart problem), and, as of ch 29, Xiaoyu, who joined as "Typhoon" after beating him in a duel. Xu Qing, the class monitor, is treasurer and rule-keeper, and Boss Mei signed as sponsor. The fund stands at 42.60, with 32.40 still needed for the 75-yuan October 15 entry fee. Lin Chen earns money through repairs and odd jobs, and *Game Frontline* will pay for his article only after the tournament deadline.

Permanent changes: Grandma's knee is worn (brace, cane, no heavy lifting for six weeks), and Lin Chen now calls his parents himself and speaks honestly. His father has early Spring Festival leave. Lin Chen's feet know the county hospital, and Grandma has noticed. He took Fang Hu's pulse during an episode (29 beats in fifteen seconds, one skipped). Fang Jianguo walks Lin Chen without forbidding the walks. Lin Chen learned Mei paid a 2,000-yuan raid fine, which she refuses to discuss. Streak's group now pays him for coaching, and Su Wen tutors him in math.
