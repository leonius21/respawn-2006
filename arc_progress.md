# CURRENT ARC SO FAR (Arc 1, chapters 1-10)
Lin Chen, a 31-year-old who died in 2022, wakes in September 2006 as a 15-year-old in Grandma's flat in Qingshui. He lives with her, hides his memory slips, and resolves to rebuild his life through the game he knows best. His hands are slow, though, so his skill lags behind his knowledge. His first Starlight game, under the borrowed ID of "Lao Wang", ends in a loss to Streak, with Fang Hu mocking him to the whole cafe. His parents' calls are strained, and his get-rich memories add up to a notebook page ending in "BITCON?? (watch for it)". That night, listening to Grandma snore through the wall, he accepts that it is real and decides: "If this is real, then I'm going to do it right." At school Xu Qing, the class monitor, logs him as "Suspicious," while he notices Su Wen secretly playing in Starlight's back room.

He befriends Big Bird, hauls Grandma's crates, and eases her anger over his cafe visits, though she refuses to see a doctor about her knee. He wins a rematch as Frost Maiden support, then loses a mirror duel to He Xiaoyu ("Typhoon"), who agrees to join if he beats her fairly. During a September 13 raid, the kids hide, Officer Fang Jianguo, Fang Hu's father, finds no one, and Lin Chen's slip, "Tell Ling," makes Monk ask how he knew his sister's name.

Lin Chen trades repairs for a free daily hour, on condition he walks Fang Hu home. He gathers Big Bird, Su Wen, Xiaoyu and Monk as "Starlight," which makes Mei freeze. Xu Qing discovers them, becomes treasurer and writes eleven rules, and Mei signs as sponsor. The 75-yuan October 15 tournament entry remains unfunded (6.50 yuan pooled).

Open threads: Monk's suspicion and Sunday trial, Xiaoyu's challenge, Su Wen's secret, Grandma's knee, his parents, and the 68.50 yuan still missing for the tournament.
