# MACRO ARCS

Title: Respawn: 2006. Ending tone: triumphant and warm, with a bittersweet edge.

Arc 1: Respawn (ch 1-40): Lin Chen wakes up at 15 in Qingshui with Grandma alive, walks into Starlight Net Cafe, and gathers Big Bird, Su Wen, Xiaoyu and Monk while Fang Hu shadows him and Xu Qing catches him. They win the county cafe tournament and earn an invitation to the Chengdu circuit.
Arc 2: The Chengdu Circuit (ch 41-80): The team becomes "Starlight" with Xu Qing as manager and fights through Chengdu's LAN scene, losing to Alina "WhiteDew" before beating her. Fang Hu becomes the team's stowaway mascot, and the zhongkao nearly breaks the team (Su Wen's parents); the arc ends with a Chengdu title and a first look at 16-year-old Qin Shuo on the national stage.
Arc 3: Shanghai Lights (ch 81-120): Starlight's first national tournament in Shanghai, where Lin Chen meets the young "Throne" and starts teaching Fang Hu for real. The odd fragments grow (moves his hands shouldn't know, a dream of an arena), and the arc ends with the earthquake on May 12, 2008, which Lin Chen's warnings soften but cannot stop.
Arc 4: After the Shaking (ch 121-160): Qingshui rebuilds, the Olympics pass, and the crisis brings Lin Chen's parents home for good while Fang Hu joins as the team's substitute. Boss Mei says something she cannot know, and a first pro contract offer forces the question of leaving town and Grandma.
Arc 5: The Seventh Time (ch 161-200): Starlight enters the pro league and runs into Golden Wolf, the club that killed Fang Hu in the original life. At the midpoint (around ch 180) Lin Chen's first recovered lost-loop memory is Fang Hu's funeral, and Boss Mei admits this is the seventh time.
Arc 6: Lost Saves (ch 201-240): Lin Chen digs up the erased loops and their failures, the worst being the loop where he won the world title and it still reset. Hurt by something Lin Chen does to protect him, Fang Hu leaves for a rival team.
Arc 7: The New Game (ch 241-280): AotA 2 launches and much of Lin Chen's future knowledge stops working on the new engine and patches. Starlight plays the first World Championship in Cologne with Fang Hu on the other side.
Arc 8: Starlight's Secret (ch 281-320): The truth comes out: Fang Hu is Mei's son and the loop is hers, and Lin Chen feels used. The team cracks and rebuilds, and Lin Chen finally commits to one role.
Arc 9: Coming Home (ch 321-360): Fang Hu comes back, his heart condition is found and he is benched, and he feels left behind again. The danger date nears while Lin Chen learns to be the person who stays.
Arc 10: Respawn Denied (ch 361-400): The 2012 World Championship in Shanghai, final against Qin Shuo's team on Fang Hu's danger date. Lin Chen walks out mid-series to find Fang Hu alone in a net cafe and save him, and the team he built wins without him; the loop breaks and the future opens.

## The Game Frontline column ("Qingshui Laoren", the Old Man of Qingshui)
- Arc 1: Lin Chen writes his first AotA strategy article; the first money order arrives near the county tournament fee.
- Arcs 1-3: the column becomes known nationwide. Its strategies spread, so rival teams learn them and his future knowledge loses value faster (show this cost on the page).
- Arc 2-3: Qin Shuo reads it in Shanghai and comes looking for the "old master"; he finds a 15-year-old. This is how the rival enters Lin Chen's life.
- Much later (arc 6+): Lantern, AotA's anonymous mapmaker, writes a letter to the magazine about one of the articles.

## Fang Hu's thread, arc by arc (he appears in every arc; never let him vanish for long)
- Arc 1 (age 11-12): the annoying little shadow at Starlight. Copies Lin Chen, gets chased out by Boss Mei, hides under desks during his father's raids. Comic, loud, the readers' favorite. First small clue: he gets dizzy after running and laughs it off.
- Arc 2 (12): the team's unofficial mascot and errand boy. Begs to come to Chengdu and sneaks onto the bus once. Lin Chen keeps saying no; Fang Hu keeps showing up anyway.
- Arc 3 (12-13): Lin Chen starts teaching him, half by accident, and finds stubborn raw talent. He practices alone at night and gets better too fast. The earthquake: he is with Lin Chen when it hits.
- Arc 4 (13-14): joins Starlight as the substitute, the youngest player in the scene. Starts taking it too seriously (too many hours).
- Arc 5 (14-15): Golden Wolf's Hou Desheng tries to sign him with big promises. Midpoint: Lin Chen's first lost-loop memory is his funeral.
- Arc 6 (15-16): Lin Chen protects him too hard (benches him, makes choices for him, like Loop 3). Hurt and feeling like a pity case, Fang Hu leaves for a rival team.
- Arc 7 (16): plays against Starlight at the Cologne World Championship, as a rival who wants to prove he's enough.
- Arc 8 (16-17): he always knew Mei is his mother; now Lin Chen and the team learn it too. His old anger comes out: at Mei for "leaving", at his father for never being home, at Lin Chen for treating him like a kid.
- Arc 9 (17): comes back to Starlight. His heart condition is found and he's benched; he feels left behind again and trains in secret.
- Arc 10 (17): the final day. He goes missing; Lin Chen finds him alone in a net cafe and saves him.

## The loops (author's notes for the planner; never on the page before the arc named)
- Original life: Fang Hu died in summer 2012, aged 17, alone in a net cafe while playing for Golden Wolf. On the night of **December 20, 2022**, Mei found the broke, 31-year-old Lin Chen dying of cold in his rented room above the hardware shop, where Starlight used to be, and wished over him: "Send back the one he was waiting for." The loop runs through Lin Chen; Mei only remembers.
- Reset rule: the condition is about FANG HU, the one who time-travels is LIN CHEN. If Fang Hu has died by December 20, 2022 (the night of Mei's wish), time resets that day, whatever Lin Chen has done and whether Lin Chen is alive or not. Lin Chen wakes up again in his 15-year-old body on the morning of September 2, 2006 (the day he first walked into Starlight, the day Fang Hu first saw him), with only his ORIGINAL life's memories. If Fang Hu is alive, there is no reset and time goes on.
- Loop 1: Lin Chen used future knowledge to become a superstar fast and won the world title, but ignored Fang Hu and lost his friends. Fang Hu died in 2012. Reset.
- Loop 2: Mei banned Fang Hu from Starlight. He went to a worse cafe across town and died a year earlier, in 2011.
- Loop 3: Mei told Lin Chen the whole truth on day one. He bodyguarded the boy like a project; Fang Hu felt like a pity case, ran away, and died anyway. Since then Mei only hints.
- Loop 4: the team broke apart because of Lin Chen's ego, and Fang Hu was left with no one.
- Loop 5: Mei hid the Chengdu invitation so Lin Chen would never go pro and leave. The team broke up; Lin Chen stayed in town, bitter, and ignored the boy. Fang Hu chased the dream alone, joined Golden Wolf, and died.
- Loop 6 (the closest; a rehearsal of this story's ending): Fang Hu was a Starlight starter until his heart condition was found days before the 2012 final. Lin Chen benched him and left him at the hotel, thinking he was safe. The night before, Fang Hu asked to talk; Lin Chen said, "After the final, Hu Zi. I promise." Starlight won the title; Fang Hu, feeling replaced, slipped out to a net cafe to play alone and died before dawn. Hearing "after the final" later triggers this memory.
- The constant in every death: different place, hour and cause each time, but he was always alone. The root: he believes he is never enough and that everyone leaves him.
- Loop 7 is this story.
