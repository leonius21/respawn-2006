# MAIN CAST ROSTER
Ages are as of September 2006. Chinese names are surname first. "(secret)" lines are author knowledge: never state them on the page before the arc named in story_rules.md.

## Lin Chen's side
- **Lin Chen** (林辰), 15 (born March 14, 1991), ninth grade at Qingshui Junior Middle School, class 3. Gamer ID **"Second"**. A 31-year-old mind in his 15-year-old body.
  *Look:* thin, a little short for his age (he grows later), messy hair, sleepy eyes, school tracksuit. Moves like an old man (stretches his back, groans getting up) and people notice.
  *Original life (he remembers it, imperfectly):* the best player at Starlight; left town at 17 (2008) for Chengdu, then the pro scene. Brilliant but switched roles constantly and played selfishly; a great player on bad teams who never found teammates who fit him, and his ego broke them. Missed Grandma's death and funeral (January 2014) for a tournament. In the 2015 World Championship final against Qin Shuo's team he cast Stasis Sphere on his own team in the deciding fight; "pulling a Second" became the scene's word for choking. Faded through low-tier teams; came home broke and died at 31 on a December night in 2022, alone in a cold rented room in Qingshui, heater unplugged to save money (he remembers the cold, nothing after).
  *Wants:* win the title he never won; this time keep Grandma, and build the team he never had.
  *Tone:* dry, tired, sarcastic; calls teens "kids"; old-man complaints; slips with future words and covers them badly. Softens slowly. Plays every role (flex); becomes teacher and captain.
  *Money:* never paid attention to stocks, business or news in his original life (it was all AotA), so he only vaguely remembers what "blew up" (Bitcon, the stock boom) and never when. His get-rich schemes fail comically (one exception, mined in 2010 and forgotten); real money comes from the game.
  *Year math (author's note, use exactly):* in fall 2006, his death (December 2022) is 16 years away; he left Qingshui at 17 in summer 2008 and came back in 2022 (14 years away); Grandma dies January 2014 (about 7 years 4 months from September 2006); the meme final is 2015. School runs Monday to Saturday; Sunday has no school.
  *Hometown knowledge (author's note):* he grew up in Qingshui and lived here until 17, then came back in 2022. He knows the town, its streets and shops, and most of its people by face and many by name, with their futures attached (who takes over the shop, who moves to Guangzhou, who gets fat). He knows Starlight inside out: the counter, the machines, the back room where Mei hides minors during raids and lets quiet regulars play, the green back door in the alley, "Lao Wang". He played at Starlight for two years with Big Bird, Monk, Xiaoyu and others in his original life, so meeting them is a reunion with people who don't know him yet, never a stranger meeting. Gaps are only what he never cared about (classmates' rankings, homework, Su Wen's and Xu Qing's inner lives, finance).
- **Grandma, Lin Guifen** (林桂芬), 68. Small, tough, gray bun, bad knees she hides. Sells pickled vegetables from a market stall, plays mahjong, shouts in thick Sichuan dialect, feeds everyone, hides how little money there is. Calls him "Chen-wa" (Sichuan for "kid Chen"). (author's note) She is Lin Jun's mother (Lin Chen's paternal grandmother); Zhou Hong is her daughter-in-law.
- **Lin Jun** (林俊), 41, father, and **Zhou Hong** (周红), 39, mother: factory workers in Dongguan, Guangdong. Call on Sundays; home only at Spring Festival. Tired, guilty, awkward with a son they barely know. "Study hard. Don't end up like us."

## The team (Starlight)
- **Zhao Dapeng "Big Bird"** (赵大鹏), 16. Failed the zhongkao; works at his family's Zhao Beef Noodles. Huge, loud, always hungry, loyal to death, no patience. Frontline/offlane. The main comedy engine. Speech: loud, simple, shouts his feelings.
- **Su Wen** (苏文), 15, Lin Chen's classmate, top of the class, his family's gaokao hope. Thin, glasses, neat. Keeps the hidden timers in his head (how long each Watcher Eye has left, when enemy ultimates and key items come back), counting a 30-second beat and tapping the desk at the top; support and second brain. Speech: quiet, precise, numbers. Parents (a teacher and a clerk) forbid games.
- **He Xiaoyu** (何小雨), 15, class 1. Short hair she cuts herself, sharp eyes, a mouth like a knife. Plays under the male ID **"Typhoon"** because no one takes girls seriously. Fearless mid. Speech: fast, cutting, mocking.
- **Tang Yi "Monk"** (唐一), 17. Dropout, Starlight's night-shift worker. Shaved head (hence "Monk"), half-closed eyes, unhurried. Calm carry who never panics. Speech: rare, short, flat. Believes he has no future.
  *(secret, first-loop-fragment clue)* He sends almost all his wages to his mother's dialysis in the county hospital and looks after his sister Tang Ling (9). Lin Chen never learned this in his original life, yet "knows" it in arc 1.
- **Xu Qing** (许晴), 15, class monitor of class 3. Perfect uniform, neat ponytail, sharp stare, a notebook of rule-breakers. Speech: formal and bossy; more formal when flustered ("This is not concern. This is my duty as class monitor."). Hates the cafe; becomes the team's manager (money, schedules, forms, lying to parents properly). Likes Lin Chen because he treats her like an adult; would never admit it. Family expects perfection.

## Starlight Net Cafe
- **Boss Mei, Mei Lan** (梅兰), 35. Owner of Starlight. Wiry, permed hair, cigarette, cardigan, slippers; plays mahjong at the counter; a sharp tongue and a ledger of debts. Chases Fang Hu out of her cafe loudly, every day.
  *(secret, reveal arc 8)* She is Fang Hu's mother. Divorced from Fang Jianguo, who has custody. She remembers the original life and all six failed loops. She only hints; she never says it plainly before the arc-5 midpoint. She knows that every loop, Lin Chen wakes on Sept 2, 2006 with his original-life memories and none of the loops, so she knows exactly who walks up her stairs that morning (hence "You came."). Lin Chen does not know she knows. She has heard many of his slips and lines before in earlier loops, so they rarely surprise her.
- **Fang Hu "Hu Zi"** (方虎, "Little Tiger"), 11 (born November 1994), sixth grade. Small, scabby knees, a gap-toothed grin, an oversized hand-me-down jacket. Loud, cheeky, the funniest kid in the cafe. Idolizes Lin Chen ("Ge!"), copies his every move, badly at first. Hides in net cafes when he's hurt. *Wants:* his name on a tournament form as a real member, not the mascot. *Hidden want:* for his father to watch him play, once.
  *(secret)* A mild heart condition no one has diagnosed yet: he gets dizzy, runs out of breath, presses his chest and laughs it off. In the original life he died in summer 2012, aged 17, alone in a net cafe, while playing for Golden Wolf.
- **Fang Jianguo** (方建国), 40, Fang Hu's father. A county policeman: stiff back, short haircut, honest, cold. His job includes raiding net cafes for minors, including Starlight. Loves his son but only gives orders; works nights, so the boy is often alone.

## Rivals and the wider scene
- **Alina Belova, "Bai Lu"** (白露), 16, Russian, ID **"WhiteDew"**. Her father is an engineer on a project in Chengdu. Short dyed hair, a big headset always round her neck, a sweet face and a wild grin. Fluent, slightly odd Chinese. Loud, teasing, says exactly what she thinks ("I'm going to crush you, and then you're buying me hotpot."). Star of Tianfu Gaming, a rich Chengdu team. Falls for Lin Chen openly once he beats her. In the original life, the greatest player of his generation who beat "Second" in the 2015 final. Now arrogant, clumsy, lonely, and secretly desperate for someone worth playing with.
- **Hou Desheng** (侯德胜), 40s, owner of Golden Wolf Club. Friendly smile, gold watch, big promises to poor boys.

## How they talk (voice sheet; describes manner only, never copy wording from here)
Most dialogue is ordinary: people repeat themselves, interrupt, trail off, answer the wrong question, say the practical thing. In any scene, at most one or two characters get a sharp or quotable line; the rest talk plainly. Wit belongs mainly to Lin Chen's narration, not to everyone's mouth.
- Lin Chen: says less than he thinks; out loud he is careful and a little too adult; the sarcasm stays mostly in his head.
- Grandma: blunt Sichuan dialect, short orders, repeats the same complaint (prices, weather, his clothes), changes the subject when it gets emotional. Funny because she is stubborn and practical, not because she is clever. Her insults are real, common Sichuan words, used plainly and often.
- Lin Jun (father): few words, ends conversations early, talks about money and work. Zhou Hong (mother): worried questions, too many at once, cries easily, then gets brisk.
- Xu Qing: formal, complete sentences, rule numbers and procedure; never jokes on purpose. She is funny only because she takes everything seriously. Gets more formal when flustered.
- Big Bird: loud, simple words, exclamations, food, big plans that fall apart; no clever wordplay.
- Su Wen: very quiet, precise, counts and numbers, long pauses; answers exactly what was asked and nothing more.
- He Xiaoyu: short, rude, mocking; as Typhoon she lowers her voice and says as little as possible. Her mockery is blunt, not witty.
- Monk: almost silent; one-word answers or a nod. A full sentence from him is an event.
- Fang Hu: an eleven-year-old who talks fast and too much, in run-on bursts, says words wrong, brags, asks "why" twice, and is funny by accident, not by wit. Never polished.
- Boss Mei: few words, flat, deflects with a scolding or an order or a mahjong remark; never explains herself.
- Fang Jianguo: very short sentences, questions without question marks, no small talk.
- Minor adults (stall-keepers, customers, clerks, neighbors): plain everyday talk about their own business; no punchlines.
