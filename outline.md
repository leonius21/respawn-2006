- Hook / End State: Quiet. His father says, "You did right to call. Don't skip school." After he hangs up, Grandma, who heard from the doorway, says nothing and puts a bowl of rice in his hands. "Your father's voice shook," she says. "Eat." She is not angry.

## Chapter 25: Bowls

## Chapter 27: The Moon Is the Same One

## Chapter 30: Two Razes
- Objective & POV: Standard. Lin Chen's POV. Day 37 (Sunday, October 8, 2006), 8 p.m. to 9 p.m. Xiaoyu's last try. The whole team, Mei and Xu Qing watch as the record stands 1-4 in games, and a loss ends the recruitment.

Wrinkle: Fang Hu, realizing his fifth seat is gone, sulks and offers the drinks tray to nobody. Then, as the team cheers, Xu Qing turns a page in her green notebook and writes a line Lin Chen can't see, her eyes resting on him a second too long.
