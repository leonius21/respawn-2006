# VOLUMES
How export divides the book. One line per division, chapter range in brackets:
"# Volume N: Title (start-end)" for a volume, "## Part Name: Title (start-end)" for a part inside it.
Arcs in macro_arcs.md fill in any volume not listed here (Arc N = Volume N).
Public, spoiler-free volume names (author decision, 2026-10-24): readers see these in the table of contents, so they never hint at the loop, Mei's secret, the earthquake or the ending. The arc titles in macro_arcs.md stay as internal working names.
