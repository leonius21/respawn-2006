# STORY RULES
Rules specific to Respawn: 2006 that the planner and the continuity checker enforce.

## Future knowledge (Lin Chen's only "power")
- Lin Chen remembers his ORIGINAL life (2006-2022), imperfectly: big events, famous players and teams, roughly when AotA patches and AotA 2 came, the general shape of strategies that became popular. He does NOT remember exact dates, scores, prices or numbers, except things every Sichuanese knows (the earthquake: May 12, 2008, 14:28). When he leans on a memory he hedges ("sometime that spring", "I think").
- The butterfly effect: the more he changes, the less his knowledge fits. People he influenced make different choices; events near him drift. Big world events (earthquake, Olympics, crisis, Halcyon/AotA 2) still happen.
- Patches change the game: a strategy from a later version may not work in the current one. AotA 2 (2011) changes the engine and much of his knowledge stops working.
- His hands are 15. Mechanical skill (APM, last-hitting, reflexes) must be trained on the page; his edge is reading, timing, drafting, leadership.
- No system, no stat windows, no luck that solves a problem. Every win must come from things readers already saw: training, a plan, a known hero/item rule, a teammate's established strength.
- Money: Lin Chen's real income is game money, earned slowly (tournament prizes, paid strategy guides for gaming magazines, coaching, later sponsors and salaries). In his original life he ignored stocks, business and news; he only vaguely remembers things that "blew up" (the stock boom and crash, Bitcoin, a few famous companies) and never when or which. His get-rich attempts are a running comedy subplot that always fails because he remembers wrong (wrong year, wrong company, buys at the top, searches for Bitcoin before it exists). They never solve a problem, cost little, and appear rarely (about once every 15-20 chapters).
- Bitcoin did not exist before January 2009. Chapter 3 plants it: he searches for it, finds nothing, and writes "BITCOIN?? (watch for it)" in his notebook. Before 2009, searches find nothing.
- Bitcoin (the one that works, quietly): in early 2009 (arc 4), after he spots a tiny forum post about a new "digital money" and remembers his note, one late-night scene at Starlight with Monk, Lin Chen mines a modest amount of Bitcoin on the old PCs, unsure whether he has already missed the timing. He writes the key on a scrap of paper ("Maybe this will be useful someday") and genuinely forgets it; Xu Qing tapes it into her team notebook "for the records". During the story it is never used, sold or checked, and no character treats it as valuable. Readers know Bitcoin exploded, so play it as dramatic irony: two or three times across the story (spaced arcs apart, never before the 2009 mining scene) the scrap nearly gets destroyed by accident (Big Bird uses the notebook page as a coaster, Xu Qing nearly throws out "old junk" when moving, Fang Hu doodles on it), survives by luck, and nobody notices. Never wink at it in narration. What happens with it after the ending: to be decided with the user later.

## The loop (the central mystery)
- Lin Chen believes this is his first and only return. Before the arc-5 midpoint (around ch 180) he never suspects a loop; he explains fragments away (talent, luck, déjà vu).
- Fragments from erased loops come only as muscle memory, déjà vu, dreams, and knowing small things he never learned in his original life. They never give clear information before the midpoint. After it, memories return only when something in the present strongly matches a lost-loop moment (same place, same words, same song), and each memory is partial.
- Boss Mei remembers everything but only hints. Before arc 5 she never says it plainly, and no POV (including hers) states the loop, her memories, or her motive.
- Before arc 8: no one learns, says or suggests that Fang Hu is Mei's son, and the loop's cause stays hidden. Public story: Mei and Fang Hu are a cranky cafe owner and an annoying kid.
- Fang Hu's heart condition: only small physical clues before arc 9 (dizziness, breathlessness, pressing his chest, laughing it off). No diagnosis before arc 9.
- See macro_arcs.md for the lost loops and their order of reveal.

## Characters and facts the model must keep straight
- Ages in September 2006: Lin Chen 15 (birthday March 14), Fang Hu 11 (birthday in November), Big Bird 16, Su Wen 15, He Xiaoyu 15, Monk 17, Xu Qing 15, Alina 16, Qin Shuo 15, Boss Mei 35, Fang Jianguo 40, Grandma 68, Lin Jun 41, Zhou Hong 39. Everyone ages a year each year.
- He Xiaoyu is a girl playing under the male ID "Typhoon"; few people know at first.
- Alina Belova is Russian; "Bai Lu" is a Chinese nickname, "WhiteDew" her ID.
- Boss Mei is a woman (she/her); "Boss" is a nickname, not a title.
- Fang Hu lives with his father, not with Mei.
- Lin Chen's parents work in Dongguan until the 2008 crisis; before that they're home only at Spring Festival.
- Game rules, heroes and items are fixed in the world bible: a hero never uses a skill it doesn't have, and a substitute may only come in between games.

## Era and tone
- Nothing appears before its time (smartphones, WeTalk, Weibu, livestreaming). A HIGH issue if presented as normal; fine only as Lin Chen's slip that others don't understand.
- Real tragedies (the earthquake) are never played for laughs or used as a simple win for Lin Chen. His warnings help some people, not all, and cost him something (disbelief, trouble at school, suspicion).
- No romance or anything sexual involving anyone under 18. Crushes are comedy; Lin Chen deflects.
- Lin Chen knows only what he learned on the page or in his original life. The state's Knowledge Gaps list is binding; using anything on it is a HIGH issue.
- Skin tone described by comparison to food or drink: LOW issue.
- Real brand names (Nokia, QQ, Apple, Coca-Cola...) are never used; only the look-alike names in the world bible. A real brand on the page is a LOW issue.
