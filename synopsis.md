# Royal Road listing

## Synopsis

Lin Chen was a pro gamer who never won the big one. At 31 he died broke and alone.

Then he woke up in 2006, fifteen years old, in a small town in Sichuan. His grandmother is alive and yelling at him to eat his porridge. Down the street, in a smoky net cafe full of old computers, the game that ate his life is still a free fan-made map that nobody takes seriously.

He has an adult's mind, sixteen years of memories, and the clumsy hands of a kid. Last time he tried to win alone. This time he's going to build a team.

A noodle-shop giant who charges into everything. A top student who counts in his sleep. The best player in town, hiding under a hood. A silent night-shift attendant. And a gap-toothed kid who won't stop following him.

**This time, Second plays for keeps.**

---
*An esports comedy-drama set in China, 2006. No system, no stat screens: just memory, practice and stubbornness.*

## Short version (for places with a character limit)

A washed-up pro gamer dies broke at 31 and wakes up at 15 in a small Sichuan town in 2006, when his game is still played in smoky net cafes. His grandmother is alive, his hands are clumsy, and this time he's going to build the team he never had. An esports comedy-drama with no system.
