On Mid-Autumn (Friday, October 6) Starlight reopens. A rusty team loses a 4v4 to Streak's holiday group, then drills under Su Wen's cooldown grid (he practiced by timing his father's tea) and wins a slow rematch, while Mei lets Lin Chen sit at fourteen till five in exchange for reviving machine 22. Fang Hu, dusting the counter for a jiao, finds a pink culture bureau receipt under the ashtray and reads it aloud ("Ge, what's a penalty?"): 2,000 yuan for admitting minors, the business suspended Oct 2-5, paid Oct 2. Lin Chen hides it again before Mei returns, then offers her the fund's 23.60 (Xu Qing logs "Purpose: pending"), and Mei shoves it back: "Sponsorship doesn't take sponsorship" (logged "Declined"). On the roof at moonrise, over five-kernel mooncakes, Mei drops one into Fang Hu's hood and puts a lotus-seed mooncake in Lin Chen's hand ("The moon's the same one it always was. Eat."), and he eats it without a word. Between the games and the roof, Lin Chen writes a long plan on a calendar page (equipment, honest money, tournaments, training for each player); Su Wen points at the spring Chengdu line, says the zhongkao comes first and his father will never sign for Chengdu, and adds quietly, "You wrote me down for next summer"; under item 5 Lin Chen writes only "Hu Zi".
