On Saturday Lin Chen, having skipped Starlight on Thursday and Friday to save money, plays Big Bird's rematch with his "real players" (Streak, Deng Wei and Liu Bo, the bespectacled "Liu boy from the third building") and wins as a calling Frost Maiden support thanks to a rune timing and a perfectly timed Big Bird charge, then leaves them arguing over who the cafe's ghost legend "Typhoon" is. He walks to the hooded kid at corner machine 38, whom he knows from his first life to be He Xiaoyu of class 1, and loses a Soul Fiend mirror duel 0-2 because his hands are slow; she mocks him savagely, then grabs his wrist and threatens to break his fingers when he quietly says her name. When he tells her he wants a team, she sets her price: beat her once, fairly, with his brain, and she'll listen. He accepts and walks away grinning.
