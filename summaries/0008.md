On Wednesday, September 13, Lin Chen (now with 3.90 yuan after a Sunday of hauling soybeans for the tofu man) is at Starlight when Boss Mei leaves "for the bank" after it has closed. He meets Monk again, and soon after, Old Tan's doorbell signals a police and culture bureau raid. Xiaoyu spots the second man in the alley, so the kids hide behind the noodle boxes in the back room instead of fleeing; Big Bird, too big to hide, becomes a customer buying Uncle Kong, and Lin Chen blurts "Tell Ling I'll fix her bike" while handing Monk a fallen chain, then hides under machine 14 with a panting, chest-pressing Fang Hu as Officer Fang Jianguo walks the rows, inspects the back room, and leaves without catching anyone. Afterward Su Wen sees Lin Chen and slips out the green door, Fang Hu laughs off his pounding heart and runs home, and when Lin Chen explains the name as a lucky guess, Monk, eyes wide open, asks how he knows his sister's name.
