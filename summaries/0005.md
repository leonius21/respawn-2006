Lin Chen walks Qingshui's main street seeing everyone's future, eats at Zhao Beef Noodles, where Big Bird (whom he wrote off as a feeder in his first life, and who gave him free noodles in 2022) boasts and invites him to a game, and he books machine 14 at Starlight from six to seven daily, without asking Mei about the back room where Su Wen is playing. In a Lianzhan game Lin Chen deliberately stops trying to control Big Bird and sees his instinct for charging at the right moment, until Lao Zhao reads Mei's timed slip, drags his son out by the ear and knocks out the power mid-game. Lao Zhao gives Lin Chen a free apology bowl, Su Wen leaves counting his coins, and Big Bird shouts that Lin Chen is "not bad for a skinny kid" and demands a Saturday rematch with "real players," which Lin Chen is glad to let him think was his idea.
