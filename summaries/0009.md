On Thursday, Day 13, Lin Chen trades repairs and virus cleaning at Starlight for a free daily hour on machine 14, and Boss Mei agrees instantly on one condition: he must walk Fang Hu to the corner by the rice shop once the streetlights come on. He fixes Ling's bike chain and tells Monk a bad "small town, everybody talks" lie, which Monk doesn't believe but lets drop, agreeing to one trial game on Sunday at 4:30. Then he gathers Big Bird, Su Wen, Xiaoyu (as "Typhoon") and Monk in the back room, where roles are argued out and Fang Hu names the team "Starlight," which makes Mei freeze and say "Don't be late for anything." They resolve to enter the October 15 tournament (50 yuan, guardian signature required) but have no adult to sign, Fang Hu cheerfully offers his policeman father, and Lin Chen walks him home.
