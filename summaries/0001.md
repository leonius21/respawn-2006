Lin Chen wakes in Grandma's flat behind the market, still cold from dying at 31 in 2022, and confirms from the tear-off calendar, the CRT TV and Grandma's glued Novia flip phone that it is Saturday, September 2, 2006. Grandma decides he has a fever and feeds him two boiled eggs and pickled-radish porridge. He slips by telling her to "check it on your phone" and covers with the 12121 weather line, and he cries into the radish he spent years trying to find again; she doesn't buy his "chili" excuse but doesn't press. She gives him 10 yuan and orders him to stay away from the net cafe above the hardware shop, and Lin Chen recognizes the origin story he told journalists for years and heads to Starlight on purpose, planning to spend only four yuan and return the rest.
