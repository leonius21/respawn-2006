On Sunday, Day 16, Lin Chen takes the second parents' call of this life on Grandma's front steps and, by refusing to fill the silences, lets his father finish a whole sentence for the first time (foreman Lao Ou might let them leave early for Spring Festival) and asks his mother what the canteen serves, which nobody had ever asked her. Grandma hijacks the phone for two minutes of rain and radish prices to bury his mother's question about her knee, then sits beside him; he promises top 25 at midterms, his father reminds him he once promised top 20, his mother offers a secret twenty yuan and he refuses it. The call runs eleven minutes and ends with his father's "Don't ruin your eyes"; Lin Chen wraps the Novia, washes the rice unasked, eats lunch with Grandma and leaves at one for Starlight to watch Streak's group before the 4:30 game.
