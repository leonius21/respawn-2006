On Wednesday, October 4, Xu Qing turns up at Grandma's door and negotiates Lin Chen and Fang Hu into a day replacing Zhao Beef Noodles' absent dishwasher for twelve yuan cash ("Receipt (disputed)"), while Grandma stays home for her son's noon call and sends a message that Lao Zhao's soup is too salty. Lin Chen washes bowls under the name "cold noodle," Fang Hu shouts orders and burns his finger on the pot ("Stood up too fast"), and Big Bird, silent and flawless at the hot line, cools the boy's hand under the faucet; in the dead hour Lao Zhao answers the soup complaint with "Good." At closing Xu Qing counts the coins twice and finds 11.90, Big Bird shamefacedly offers his own jiao, Lao Zhao nods at him, returns it and pays the jiao himself, the fund reaches 23.60 (51.40 remaining), and Lao Zhao hands Lin Chen a bowl from the bottom of the pot and says "My rice bucket comes home every night. Not many do," while Big Bird scrubs an already-clean lid.
