On Sunday, after Grandma accepts his notebook lie about the missing money, Lin Chen hears his parents for the first time in this life on Grandma's Novia. His gentleness alarms his mother ("Did you break something? Do you need money?"), and his father ends the call at seven minutes with "Study hard. Don't end up like us." That evening at Starlight he drills last-hits alone on an empty map, plots a future fortune, and finds that Baidoo has nothing on Bitcoin and that he remembers almost nothing useful; Fang Hu drops by, asks "who's winning," and gets chased out. At home he writes a list of question-marked half-memories ending in "BITCOIN?? (watch for it)," admits the game is all he knows, and promises himself two hours of drilling a day and better Sunday calls.
