On Sunday afternoon, Day 16, Lin Chen stands behind Streak's group for three hours studying their habits (Streak takes every rune; all five dive at any bleeding hero), then swaps his free hour to 4:30 for Monk's trial game in the back room. Despite Su Wen's slow count in the first minutes (a Watcher Eye ran out early on him), Big Bird taking the sleeping Fang Hu's chair (Fang Hu sulks until he's made "drinks man"), and Xiaoyu watching as an observer while pretending not to, Lin Chen baits Streak's five at the 16-minute rune into Big Bird's Tidecaller ultimate, lands two of three razes, and Monk's Phantom Blade cleans up four kills on the way to a 31-minute win. Monk finishes with the highest gold on the map, stands, and tells the floor "Next Sunday. Same time.", the first full sentence he has volunteered to Lin Chen in either life.
