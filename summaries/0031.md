On Tuesday, October 10, Lin Chen spends Grandma's lunch money on an hour at machine 14 to search Baidoo about a child's fast, skipping pulse, finds only the vague "usually harmless, see a doctor if dizziness or fainting," fails comically at searches for stocks and Bitcon while hiding the screen from Fang Hu, and gives his last twenty minutes to Fang Hu so he can watch the boy's color. On Wednesday at the school library study group, Su Wen reads the 1988 Home Medical Guide aloud without asking why ("Dizziness. Fainting. Chest pain on exertion."), Xu Qing silently writes something after seeing the word "fainting," and Big Bird sleeps on Du Fu, then wakes to correctly declare that the poet is sad about the war. Walking home at dusk, Lin Chen is stopped at the rice-shop curb by a sourceless certainty that he once sat there in the rain waiting for someone who never came; he sits on the dry curb, blames déjà vu and exam nerves, and decides to ask Fang Jianguo plainly on Friday after the last exam.
