On Monday at school, Lin Chen learns he ranked 34th last semester (Su Wen first, Xu Qing second), and class monitor Xu Qing logs his dozing, old-man sighs, grandfather posture, blank math homework and a "WeTalk" slip in her green notebook. Called to the board by Teacher Gao, he instantly solves a pig-farm growth-rate problem, then can't factor the easy follow-up, and his clumsy "pickle brine" cover earns him "Suspicious" in Xu Qing's notebook. He notices Su Wen counting to thirty under his breath (the creep-wave timer) and glimpses a rune-timings grid in his Chinese textbook; after school he follows Su Wen, who slips through Starlight's green back door in the alley with a carefully counted stack of one-yuan coins.
