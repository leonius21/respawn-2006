On Wednesday night's shift (Oct 11-12), Monk checks IDs at the top of Starlight's stairs, mops, restocks the fridge, lets his sister Ling sleep on the back-room sofa under Mei's silent tolerance, takes his week's wages in Mei's envelope, and counts fifteen yuan out of his tea tin for his unpaid tournament share before putting it back, the hospital paper (window three, Mon/Wed/Fri, an underlined amount) still in his shoe. At 1:20 a drunk Lao Pan offers a bathhouse tag as ID; Monk's "Card, or stairs" and stare wake Ling, Mei dismisses the man as a "xianggong," and in the quiet hours Monk briefly recalls that Lin Chen knew Ling's name unasked and reads Xu Qing's taped tournament sheet, placing each teammate for Sunday. At 5:40 a.m. he folds the fifteen yuan into the toe of his sock, Ling clips on and rings her rabbit bell, and he walks the bike through the waking market toward the school and hospital roads, ringing the bell once himself.
