On Friday, Day 21, Xu Qing chairs a formal budget meeting in Starlight's back room with an agenda: Big Bird's noodle-coupon plan collapses when he realizes the "kitchen" is his father, Su Wen goes silent when asked for more money and Lin Chen covers for him, Typhoon refuses to pay (or lower the hood) until he's actually on the team, and Lin Chen's remembered "third card wins" scratch card, bought with his own Sunday money, turns up a peach, which Xu Qing logs as "fined." Fang Hu pours out a chipped toffee tin of bottle money and New Year coins; Lin Chen's reflexive "No kid money" crushes him, so they settle on three yuan with a real receipt. The fund reaches 7.60, Xu Qing writes "Remaining: 67.40 (pending)," and Fang Hu tucks the receipt inside his jacket like a medal as the streetlights come on.
