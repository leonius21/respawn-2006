On Thursday, Day 20, at Starlight, Lin Chen finds *Game Frontline*'s call for AotA strategy (100 yuan per thousand characters, paid by money order after publication) and gives up his free hour to write a mid-lane article on rune timing and wave control, leaving out his real read on opponents; Su Wen edits it into numbered points on composition paper (1,582 characters), Fang Hu names the author "Qingshui Laoren," and Xu Qing objects but logs it as an "Investment (pending)." Because a ninth grader can't cash a money order and the postman's wife plays mahjong with Grandma, Boss Mei agrees to receive the payment under her name at Starlight for ten percent, Monk seals the envelope with a grain of rice, and Xu Qing pays for the paper, envelope and stamps, leaving the fund at 4.60 yuan. At the green post box by the market, Lin Chen hesitates before letting the envelope drop, and Fang Hu gives it a full Young Pioneer salute "for the old man."
