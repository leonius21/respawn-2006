On Wednesday, at the tea-house mahjong table, Old Tan's wife Qiu Fang tells Grandma that Lin Chen climbs the stairs to Starlight every evening; Grandma plans a furious lecture, throws away her winning tile and loses one yuan fifty. That evening Lin Chen carries her crates home at her pace, watches her on the steps, thanks her for dinner like a grown man, which kills the lecture, and asks about her knee and a county hospital joint doctor, which she refuses ("Okay for now"). He finally factors his math and then draws lane-and-rune diagrams on the backs of the calendar pages he now tears daily; she leaves him a bowl of fermented rice soup with egg without asking and lies awake counting his steady breathing.
