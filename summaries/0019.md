At 8:25 on Sunday, Day 23, Officer Fang Jianguo walks Lin Chen along the dark river path and, in short sentences, asks only about his son: whether he runs, whether he gets out of breath (Lin Chen tells him about yesterday's dizziness at the curb; Fang Jianguo says "He always says that"), and whether he talks too much. Lin Chen answers truthfully, says nothing about the back room, and tells him about Fang Hu's two-sentence essay; Fang Jianguo crouches to retie the broken, knotted lace on his polished shoe and never confirms or denies what he saw on the night of the raid. At the bridge steps he orders "Home by nine. Both of you." and leaves, and Lin Chen realizes the man hasn't said no to the walks, resolves to be at the corner tomorrow, and runs home.
