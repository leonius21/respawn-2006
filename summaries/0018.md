On Sunday evening, Day 23, Lin Chen pays two yuan (earned unloading the tofu man's cart at dawn) for Xiaoyu's first weekly Soul Fiend mirror duel at machines 37-38, with Xu Qing observing "the conditions of recruitment" and Streak's group watching after Mei charges each of them a yuan to "look at the ghost." He loses game one to his own slow fingers, wins game two with a wave-and-rune trap built around her habit of keeping one raze back, and loses game three when she copies his method with faster hands; Mei hands the four yuan to Xu Qing as "sponsorship" (fund now 11.60, 63.40 remaining), and Xiaoyu tells him he "played like you'd watched me for a year" and promises to keep two razes next week. At 8:25 he comes down the stairs to find Officer Fang Jianguo in plain clothes waiting at the rice-shop corner, who says his name and tells him to walk with him.
