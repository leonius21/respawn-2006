On Saturday, Day 22, Xu Qing holds the first weekly grade check in Starlight's back room: Lin Chen's math quiz is a "passable" 61, Big Bird copies Fang Hu's arithmetic page (tiny tiger faces included) until Fang Hu blocks question seven, and when Xu Qing scolds him formally about fractions, Big Bird surprises everyone by asking her to explain it again, then solves the next problem himself. Fang Hu's essay "My Father" reads only "My father is a policeman. He is at work."; Lin Chen reads it and says nothing, giving up his free hour on machine 14 to stay. At the rice-shop corner, Fang Hu goes dizzy for a second at the curb, presses his chest and laughs it off as standing up too fast, then runs, turns and waves with his whole arm, and Lin Chen finds himself already waving back.
