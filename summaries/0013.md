In the library during activity class they trade (Lin Chen fixes Su Wen's Tidecaller timer: "start from cast, not from sight"), until Teacher Su walks in, recognizes the boy whose size-34 sneaker he still keeps in his shoe cabinet, and, delighted that his son is "helping a struggling classmate," declares a regular Wednesday study group at that table, while Xu Qing logs it all in her green notebook and invokes rule five. At the corner after school Su Wen whispers, "He believes it, because it is partly true," and Lin Chen laughs for the first time that day.
