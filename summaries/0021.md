On Sunday, October 1, from Boss Mei's point of view, she counts out the money for the fine Fang Jianguo wrote up the night of the raid, leaves the sealed public security bureau envelope unopened, and chases a bored Fang Hu out (he reports Grandma's fall). Lin Chen arrives ten minutes late for the Monk game with brine on his knees and plays distracted; Monk carries the win. Lin Chen shuts down Fang Hu's offer to carry Big Bird's father's soup to Grandma with "You're a kid. Stay out of it," skips machine 14, and comes back soaked at eight, where Xiaoyu beats him 2-0 keeping two razes ("Next week. Bring your whole head") while Mei charges Streak's group a yuan each for the till. Mei starts to say something to him, turns it into a scolding about his wet sleeves, flinches at the window when he yanks Fang Hu back from an unlit motorbike at the rice-shop corner, and after closing sits with the cash tin, the sealed envelope and a seven-of-dots tile before turning off the neon and climbing the stairs one at a time.
