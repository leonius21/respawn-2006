On Monday, October 2, Lin Chen takes Grandma's key at 5:30 a.m. and runs the pickle stall, drawing a line with free tastes, getting corrected by Postman Wu's wife when he quotes a 2022 price, and seeing Boss Mei in real shoes heading toward the county offices. Grandma arrives at ten on her broom crutch, coaches him from her stool, demolishes his plan to take over the stall ("once is a present, every day is taking it"), and charms hotel buyer Ayi Liu into paying full price for the half of her reserved black jar that Lin Chen hadn't given away as samples; Lin Chen apologizes to Fang Hu, who brings Lao Zhao's soup and outsells him by shouting about "the Old Man of Qingshui." At dusk Grandma counts a best-in-years 61.40, ties a roll for Tuesday's doctor into a handkerchief, and pays him two yuan in wages, which he accepts, then he walks her home slowly on his shoulder.
