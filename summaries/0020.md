On Sunday, October 1 (golden week day 1), Monk tells Lin Chen at the pickle stall that a letter has come, and at Starlight Boss Mei hands him an envelope she "kept under the ashtray": Game Frontline will print his article in the November issue and pay by money order after publication; on the stairs he finds a handwritten notice, CLOSED OCT 2–5, and Mei refuses to explain. Running back to the market, he arrives seconds too late as Grandma's knee gives out under a crate of chili cabbage; she orders him not to tell his parents, so he asks Xu Qing (at the market buying eggs) for a rule-five ruling, and she approves "silence (until doctor)." He carries Grandma home on his back while she tells him that coming once is easy and staying is what matters, and she agrees to Tuesday's county-hospital bone doctor if he carries her bag and never skips school for her leg; that afternoon he pins the letter above his bed, realizes the money will arrive long after the tournament fee is due, and, after Grandma declares "okay for now" over, counts ten usable days out of fourteen and goes to wash the rice.
