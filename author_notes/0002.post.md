**A few notes on this chapter:**

- **Borrowed IDs:** net cafes had to register every customer's ID card, and minors weren't allowed in. So kids typed in an adult's ID number. In Qingshui, every kid uses the number of the same laid-off worker, Wang Jianjun.
- **Young Pioneers:** almost every Chinese schoolkid belongs to this children's organization and wears its **red scarf** at school. Fang Hu wears his stuffed in a pocket.
- **Uncle Kong** is this story's version of the most famous instant-noodle brand in China. Every net cafe sold it.
