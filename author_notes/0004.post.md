**A few notes on this chapter:**

- **WeTalk** is this story's version of WeChat, the chat app everyone in China uses today. It launched in 2011, so in 2006 nobody has heard of it. That's why Xu Qing writes the word down.
- **Eye exercises:** Chinese schools stop every day for a few minutes of eye massage to music from the loudspeaker. Every kid who grew up there remembers the counting.
- **Class monitor:** a student chosen to keep order in the class for the teacher. Xu Qing takes the job very seriously.
- **Rankings:** test results are ranked, and everyone knows everyone's place. "34th" is not a good number.
