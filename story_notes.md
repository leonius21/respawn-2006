## Planning decisions so far
- First life: a very talented pro who never won the biggest title (choke / team breakup / scandal), ending as a washed-up veteran around 30, forgotten in low-tier teams.
- Goes back to age ~15-16, into the era when AotA is still young (a mod played in cyber cafes, no real pro scene yet). He knows it will become huge.
- Why he goes back: a mystery with a real answer, revealed late as a twist.
- Setting: a country where cyber cafes are a teen thing (exact country not final yet).
- Country: China, real cities and real era (only the games, platforms and companies are fictional). The story can expand to other countries later (international scene).
- Era: he goes back to about 2006, when AotA is a mod for a fictional strategy game; the standalone "AotA 2" with huge prize money arrives about 5-7 years later. His first life ended around 2022.
- Tone: grounded esports drama with warmth and a LOT of comedy; a few satisfying payoff scenes, wins are earned, opponents and adults aren't stupid.
- No system. His edges: adult mind, imperfect memories of the future, pro game sense. His 15-year-old hands need training. Rules: future knowledge loses value as he changes things (butterfly effect); memory is imperfect (big events, not exact dates/numbers); patches change the game.
- Family: "left-behind child". Parents are migrant workers in a far city; he lives with his grandmother in a small town. The family struggles for money (no debt). First-life regret: grandma died while he was away chasing tournaments.
- First-life career: he switched roles often (one reason he never won) and played selfishly: a great player on bad teams who never found teammates who fit him. His ego broke teams, then a famous choke in the biggest final became a meme and finished him.
- This life: his adult mind makes him the one who builds, teaches and leads the team.
- Personality: tired, sarcastic "old man" in a teen body who slowly softens (comedy from old-man habits, future slang, forgotten homework), with some shameless use of future knowledge. Heart arc: learning to trust a team.
- Role now: still a flex player, which becomes his strength as teacher/captain; maybe he finally commits to one role in a late arc.
- Rivals: loose and changing. The man who beat him in the meme final is a kid in 2006 and the first rival, but rivals can turn into friends and friends into rivals; new rivals keep arriving.
- Mystery: the user likes the LOOP idea (he has looped before and failed; the loop does not end by winning). Being developed; details not final.
- Loop rules (agreed draft): he always wakes on the same morning in 2006; he always keeps the memories of his ORIGINAL life; the loops in between are erased except fragments (muscle memory, deja vu, dreams, knowing things he never learned); the reset comes on a fixed date in 2022 no matter what, even if he won. Midpoint reveal (arc ~5): he has looped before (about the 7th time). One loop he won the world title and it still reset.
- Anchor: the old cyber cafe owner remembers the loops; she only hints (telling him directly broke things in an earlier loop). Looks like comic background at first.
- Why the loop exists and how it ends (B+C): the loop belongs to the cafe owner. Her son died in the original timeline; her wish to undo her son's death pulled our hero back (her son idolised him). The exit is saving the boy, which needs everything the hero learns (see people, teach, stay, care more about the team than the trophy). The danger date lines up with the biggest final of the story.
- The boy (name TBD): ~11-12 in 2006, the cafe owner's son, but nobody connects them (divorced parents, lives with strict father, father's surname; mother and son act like strangers in public, she scolds and chases him out as a comedy bit). Loud, cheeky, funniest kid in the cafe; idolises the hero ("Ge"), copies his moves.
  - Original life: hero left at 17 and never looked back; the boy followed alone, trained to exhaustion, joined a team that exploited kids, collapsed and died in a cafe around 17 after a very long session. Hero barely remembered his name.
  - Health: a mild hidden heart condition ("fine if he rests") plus overwork, pressure and loneliness. Early clues: dizziness, out of breath.
  - Four stages: (1) arcs 1-2 comic sidekick/shadow, readers' favourite; (2) arcs 3-4 student, youngest member or substitute of the team; (3) arc 5 midpoint: hero's first recovered lost-loop memory is the boy's funeral; (4) arcs 6-10: each lost loop shows a different death from the same root (he feels never enough, everyone leaves him); bodyguarding fails, the hero must be the person who stays; the danger date lines up with the biggest final, and the hero makes the right choice this time.
- The game: AotA = "Attack of the Ancients", a mod for "Peacecraft III", a fantasy strategy game by "Snowstorm Entertainment". Very close to DotA (5v5, three lanes, jungle, towers, the Ancient, last-hitting, gold, items, ~100 heroes) but with invented hero and item names; hero names closely resemble their real counterparts without copying them. Keep a named hero/item list in the world bible as hard rules.
- Game detail in prose: readable for non-gamers (feelings, pressure, key moments, short sharp play-by-play, then reactions; fights like action scenes), with one or two technical "nerd moments" per match, each explained in one line.
- Protagonist: Lin Chen (林辰, "chen" linked to time). Gamer ID "Second" (always second place / second chance). First-life meme: "pulling a Second" = choking in a final.
- Hometown: a small county town in Sichuan (spicy food, mahjong grannies, local dialect comedy). Parents work in factories in Guangdong. Path: town cafe -> Chengdu -> Shanghai -> the world.
