# Royal Road release schedule: Respawn: 2006

How to read this: each **release** is one posting day. A release is one chapter, or two chapters that belong together (same day in the story, or one sets up and the next pays off). A chapter that ends on a big cliffhanger is always released **alone and last**, so readers wait a day for the answer. A chapter that answers a cliffhanger starts the next release.

## Rhythm

- **Launch day:** chapters 1-5 together. Royal Road readers judge a new story fast, and five chapters (about 13,700 words) is enough to show the hook (dead pro wakes up at 15), the world (Grandma, Starlight) and the first teammates.
- **Weeks 1-2:** one release every day, at the same time each day. New stories climb Rising Stars on steady daily updates and follower spikes in the first two weeks. A good time is US evening, about 9-11 a.m. China time.
- **After that:** 5 releases a week (Monday to Friday), same time. Switch earlier if the stock of finished chapters ahead of the release drops below 10.
- **Always keep a buffer** of at least 10 accepted chapters that aren't posted yet. Royal Road can schedule chapters ahead, so the whole buffer can be loaded at once.
- Post the culture guide (`author_note_ch1.html`) as Chapter 1's author note. Tick the "Profanity" content warning.

## Rules for future chapters

- After each accepted chapter, look at how it ends:
  - A big cliffhanger is released alone.
  - Quiet or short chapters pair with the next one when they share a day or a thread.
  - Big action or emotional peaks (tournament games, reveals) get their own release.
- A tournament day split over several chapters can go out as a 2-3 chapter release (a weekend "event drop"), but never cut a match in half between releases unless it's a deliberate cliffhanger.
- Never release two cliffhanger chapters back to back on consecutive days. Put a calmer release between them.
